# Store listing — English

Name: **b4Play — summaries before you play**

Category: **Productivity**

Summary: **Read YouTube transcript summaries of up to 200 words before you choose what to watch.**

Description:

b4Play adds a compact summary card next to YouTube video thumbnails. Hover over a thumbnail or open a standard video and use the b4Play toolbar button. Summaries and the interface are available in English and Brazilian Portuguese.

This version requires a local Node.js 22+ companion service and your own OpenAI or OpenRouter API key. Set them up in b4Play settings before using summaries. AI usage may incur charges from your provider.

When you request a summary, a Tactiq result tab opens in the background to retrieve the transcript, then closes. The local service saves the transcript and sends its text and the video title to your selected AI provider. The result appears in a popup and is cached locally. You can pause hover, clear the cache, check your API key, or import a .txt transcript.

Tactiq availability and supported languages can limit automatic retrieval. Shorts are not supported. The summary describes the transcript, not visual-only content. No advertising, analytics or developer-operated backend is included. This beta is independently developed and has not been approved by a browser store.
